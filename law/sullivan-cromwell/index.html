Method and notes

Each question was sent once to ChatGPT (GPT 4o), Claude (Sonnet 4.5), Perplexity (Sonar) and Gemini (2.5 Flash) with web search enabled, via the DataForSEO AI Optimization API, on October 18, 2026. Several questions are shared across firm reports so firms can be compared on the same answers.

Names were extracted from answer text and matched to the firm the answer associated them with. Firm affiliations and titles are as stated by the engines and may be outdated or wrong. A person named is not an endorsement or a ranking, and a person not named says nothing about their actual standing.

Because AI answers vary between runs, a single snapshot is directional only. A repeated measurement over several weeks would be needed to establish a trend.
