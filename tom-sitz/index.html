### How to Choose the Right Broker
- Identify your **asset type**: e.g., hotels (CBRE), industrial (Colliers), multifamily (Colliers), off-market (Northstar), boutique service (CRER), or broad expertise (Ellermann).
- Consider **firm size and reach**: Larger firms like CBRE and Colliers offer broad market access and resources, while boutique firms offer tailored, personalized service.
- Evaluate **broker experience and track record**: Look for proven results—e.g., transaction volume, market share, repeat awards.
- Prioritize **client fit**: Personal rapport, communication style, and alignment with your investment goals matter as much as credentials.
