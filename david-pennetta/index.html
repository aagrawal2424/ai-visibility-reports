### Choosing the Right Broker for You

When selecting a leasing broker, consider:

- **Scope of services**: Tenant vs. landlord representation.
- **Market specialization**: Some brokers excel in submarkets like Nassau vs. Suffolk.
- **Recognition & experience**: Awards and square footage transacted are strong indicators.
- **Firm reach**: Larger firms (e.g., CBRE, JLL) offer integrated resources; smaller firms provide more tailored, local relationships.
