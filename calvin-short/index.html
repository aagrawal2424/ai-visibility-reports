### How to Choose the Right Broker

If you're evaluating which broker or firm to work with, consider the following factors:

- **Transaction Size & Specialty**: Do you need a broker with experience in high-value, institutional-level deals (e.g., Shierberl, Duong, Fryman), or someone focused on mid-size, privately held properties (e.g., Resha Group)?
- **Local Market Knowledge**: Firms like Kidder Mathews and Hanley Investment Group have deep roots in Orange County and regional insight.
- **Advisory vs. Transactional Focus**: If you value strategic portfolio guidance alongside sales execution, boutique advisory firms like The Resha Group may offer more tailored services.
- **Track Record & Recognition**: Awards and high transaction volume—such as CoStar Power Broker accolades or over $1 billion closed—can be indicators of performance and reputation.
- **Service Range**: Some brokers specialize strictly in sales, while others provide leasing, valuation, exchange structuring, and asset management.
