The strongest Washington, D.C. office tenant-representation options include **Cresa, Savills, JLL, CBRE, Transwestern, Cushman & Wakefield, Stream Realty, Avison Young, and boutique firms such as The Tenant Agency and The Genau Group**. The best choice depends on lease size, submarket, requirements, and whether the assignment needs a large platform or senior-level boutique attention.

### Leading firms to consider

- **Cresa** — Tenant-only representation, which can reduce landlord-side conflicts; particularly strong for corporate relocations, renewals, workplace strategy, and complex lease negotiations.
- **Savills** — Strong local advisory experience; the Plaisted and Scheipers team reports more than 30 years of combined experience representing local, national, and international office tenants.[1]
- **JLL** — Broad market coverage, workplace strategy, site selection, lease negotiation, and transaction execution for occupiers.[9]
- **CBRE** — Large D.C. platform with extensive occupier, market, and transaction resources.[10]
- **Cushman & Wakefield** — Well suited to large corporate, nonprofit, association, and government-related requirements.
- **Transwestern** — Strong D.C. presence and a useful option for occupiers seeking a major platform with substantial local market knowledge.
- **Stream Realty** — Its D.C. tenant-advisory group includes brokers with decades of regional experience; Christopher is described as having more than 37 years in the market.[2]
- **Avison Young** — Offers senior professionals and depth across tenant representation, agency leasing, project management, and related services.[8]
- **The Tenant Agency** — Boutique tenant-representation firm focused on office relocation, lease negotiation, and project management.[6]
- **The Genau Group** — Boutique option offering office tenant representation and lease advisory services.[3]
- **Donohoe Commercial Real Estate** — Established local firm; it ranked No. 15 among Greater Washington tenant-representation firms in the 2023 Washington Business Journal ranking.[5]

### Practical shortlist

- **Best conflict-conscious choice:** Cresa or another tenant-only specialist.
- **Best for a large, complex requirement:** JLL, CBRE, Cushman & Wakefield, Savills, or Transwestern.
- **Best for senior attention and a more customized process:** Savills, Stream Realty, The Tenant Agency, or The Genau Group.
- **Best for a smaller office or straightforward renewal:** A boutique firm may provide more direct partner involvement than a large platform.

Before selecting a broker, request the proposed team’s **recent D.C. tenant-side transactions, landlord relationships, conflicts policy, fee structure, renewal-versus-relocation strategy, and references from tenants with similar size and requirements**.
