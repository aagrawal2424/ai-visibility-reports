###  Choosing the Right Broker

- **Large Firms (C&W, CBRE, JLL)**  
  Ideal for mid-size to large tenants or landlords needing institutional-level market research, a broad deal pipeline, and resources for complex or large-scale transactions.

- **Mid-Market Local Firms (HW Enterprise, MacKenzie)**  
  Provide hands-on service, deep local market knowledge, and often more personalized attention—especially valuable for businesses focusing on specific neighborhoods like Harbor East, Federal Hill, or Towson.

- **Independent and Boutique Firms (KLNB, etc.)**  
  Offer flexibility, competitive commission structures, and may be better suited to small to mid-size leasing needs or specialized property types.
