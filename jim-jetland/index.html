**Choosing the Right Broker for You**

Here are a few tips to guide your selection:

- Clarify your business size, required office type (downtown, suburban, creative, hybrid), timeline, and strategic needs.
- Larger firms like JLL or CBRE bring extensive resources and analytics, while local or independent firms (Rokos, CRE Partners, Obsidian) offer personalized service and flexibility.

- Always verify that the broker exclusively represents tenants—not landlords—to avoid conflicts of interest, as emphasized by the City of Minneapolis and industry best practices.
