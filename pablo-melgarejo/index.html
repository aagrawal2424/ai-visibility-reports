To determine which broker or firm is the best fit for your situation, consider:

- **Deal size & complexity**: Large national firms like JLL, CBRE, or Cushman & Wakefield are well-suited for multi-market or headquarters relocations.

- **Submarket focus**: For deep local expertise, consider Cumberland & Worthy or NAI Brannen Goddard.
- **Specialized service**: Colliers offers a broad suite of services, including sustainability, portfolio strategy, and data analytics.
- **Client experience**: Brokers like Erin Smith have handled massive assignments; Nick Fitzpatrick offers long-term client partnerships; Colliers’ Pete Shelton brings longevity and consistency.
