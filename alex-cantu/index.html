**How to Choose the Right Broker for You**  
When selecting a broker, consider the following factors:

- **Property type and specialization**: Some brokers focus on industrial (e.g., Allies), others on office or multifamily (e.g., CBRE, Colliers).
- **Transaction size and complexity**: Large institutional deals may benefit from CBRE, Cushman, or Colliers, while local or nimble deals might align better with McClure or Allies.
- **Credentials and experience**: Look for industry designations (CCIM, SIOR), awards (e.g., Everest Club, IBJ honors), and years of local expertise.
- **Firm scale and reach**: National platforms like CBRE and Colliers offer extensive reach; boutique firms like Allies and McClure offer agility and personalized service.
