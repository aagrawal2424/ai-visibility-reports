##  How to Choose the Right Broker

1. **Tenant-Only vs. Dual Representation**  
   Tenant-only firms like Hughes Marino, Shelton & Associates, and Peninsula CRE eliminate conflicts of interest and prioritize your objectives.

2. **Firm Size & Resources**  
   Large firms (JLL, Cushman & Wakefield) offer broader submarket data, analytics, and support teams—ideal for complex or multi-location needs.

3. **Local Expertise**  
   Boutique firms or individual brokers (RSFLA, Shelton) often bring tailored, high-touch service and deep submarket knowledge.

4. **Track Record & Reviews**  
   Use platforms like Clutch for verified client reviews and performance benchmarks. ([clutch.co])

5. **Specialized Experience**  
   Match your business sector (e.g., creative, life sciences, tech, medical) with a broker who has relevant experience and portfolio success.
