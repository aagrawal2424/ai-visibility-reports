The strongest Manhattan office tenant-representation options depend on your company’s size, requirements, and preferred service model. A practical shortlist is:

- **Cushman & Wakefield** — Best for large occupiers, complex headquarters moves, multi-market requirements, and sophisticated lease negotiations. Its New York platform explicitly offers tenant representation and corporate real-estate services.[5][8]
- **JLL** — Strong for corporate occupiers needing workplace strategy, labor analytics, project management, and national or global coordination.
- **CBRE** — A good fit for large tenants seeking broad market coverage, portfolio strategy, and access to extensive Manhattan leasing data.
- **Newmark** — Particularly strong in New York office leasing, landlord relationships, and large or high-profile transactions.
- **Savills** — Well suited to financial, professional-services, and international firms seeking integrated tenant representation and workplace advisory.
- **Colliers** — A solid option for mid-sized and large tenants that want institutional capabilities with potentially more focused team attention.
- **Hughes Marino** — A tenant-focused firm worth considering if you want a representation model centered on occupiers rather than landlords; it specializes in commercial brokerage and corporate leasing services.[6][7]
- **Metro Manhattan** — An independent Manhattan-focused option for companies wanting a local specialist and a single broker managing the search through lease signing.[4][9]
- **SquareFoot** — Often useful for startups and small or growing companies that want help comparing available NYC office options and navigating the leasing process.[1]
- **TenantBase** — Another practical option for smaller businesses seeking tenant-rep assistance, particularly when the requirement is straightforward.[2]
