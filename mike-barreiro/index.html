### Choosing the Right Broker for You

When selecting a broker, consider:

- **Specialization & Experience**: Do they have deep industrial expertise and relevant experience in your specific submarket (e.g., Anaheim vs. Irvine)?
- **Track Record**: Look at transaction volume, deal size, and success stories that mirror your needs.
- **Client Feedback**: Testimonials and references offer valuable insight into their professionalism and results.
- **Team vs. Individual**: Some clients benefit from a dedicated individual, while others prefer the breadth of a team like Hefner & Vernick.
- **Firm Resources**: Institutional firms like Colliers, Voit, and Kidder Mathews often provide additional market intelligence and broader networks.
